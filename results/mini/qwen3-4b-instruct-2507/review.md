## m002: How does regenerative braking work in electric cars?

Answerable: True

Problems:
- low groundedness 60% (possible outside knowledge or heavy paraphrase)

Answer:

````text
Regenerative braking in electric cars works by converting kinetic energy into electrical energy when the vehicle decelerates. The electric motor acts as a generator, spinning in reverse to produce electricity that is stored in the battery [2]. This process captures energy that would otherwise be lost as heat during braking in conventional vehicles [1]. Some of the kinetic energy is used to charge the battery, while the remainder is used by the brake system to provide friction-based stopping [2]. The system is often adjustable, with some vehicles allowing users to customize the level of regenerative braking [3].
````
